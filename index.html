Questions

Frequently asked questions.

If you’re wondering whether this reset is right for you, these answers may help you decide.

Do I need to be officially “in perimenopause” to join?
No diagnosis is required. This reset is designed for women who suspect they’re in perimenopause or are already experiencing symptoms like cycle changes, sleep issues, anxiety, hot flashes, or weight shifts.
How much time will this take each day?
Most days can be completed in 20–30 minutes total, woven into your existing routine. You’ll get a clear daily focus so you can do what matters most without feeling overwhelmed.
Is this a strict diet or detox? Will I be hungry?
This is not a crash diet or deprivation plan. The focus is on nourishment, blood sugar balance, and gentle support for your hormones and nervous system—not restriction.
Can I do this if I’m on medication or HRT?
The reset is designed as a lifestyle and nutrition support framework and can often complement medical care. Always check with your healthcare provider before making changes to your routine.
Ready to feel more at home in your body again?
Perimenopause doesn’t have to feel like everything is falling apart. With the right support, this can be a season of reconnection, clarity, and deep self-care.
